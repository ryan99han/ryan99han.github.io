## ViT

![ViT](/assets/images/vit.png)

### Overview
- A standard transformer receives a 1D sequence of token embeddings
- For 2D images, the image $x \in \mathbb{R}^{H \times W \times C}$ is reshaped into a sequence of flattened 2D patches $x_p \in \mathbb{R}^{N \times (P^2 \cdot C)}$
  - $(H, W)$ is the resolution of the original image
  - $C$ is the number of channels
  - $(P, P)$ is the resolution of each image patch
  - $N = HW / P^2$ is the resulting number of patches, and also the effective input sequence length of the transformer
- The transformer uses constant latent vector size $D$ through all its layers so a trainable linear projectiion is used to flatten the patches and map to $D$ dimensions
- Like BERT's [class] token, a learnable embedding is prepended to the sequence of embedded patches
  - The state of the class token at the output of the transformer encoder serves as the image representation $y$
  - The representation $y$ is normalized and passed to a classification MLP head to output class predictions
- Standard learnable 1D position embeddings are added to the patch embeddings

### Inductive Bias
- ViT has much less image-specific inductive bias than CNNs
- In CNNs, locality, 2D neighborhood structure, and translation equivariance are baked into each layer throughout the model
- In ViT, only MLP layers are local and translationally equivariant (they are applied to each patch token vector independently), while the self-attention layers are global
- At initialization time, the position embeddings carry no information about the 2D positions of the patches and the spatial relations between patches have to be learned
  - The authors experimented with explicit 2D positional embeddings $(x, y)$ but found they gave virtually no performance improvement over 1D learnable embeddings

### Finetuning
- The ViT is pretrained on large datasets and finetuned to smaller downstream tasks
- The pre-trained prediction head is removed and a zero-initialized $D \times K$ feedforward layer is attached, where $K$ is the number of downstream classes

## Summary
- ViTs scale remarkably well with dataset size and compute
- Lower layers in ViTs immediately learn to integrate information across the entire image while also attending to local regions, which is quite distinct from early CNN layers
