All the things I do to stay sane in this crazy world...

## Phone Apps
1. Delete them (TikTok, YouTube, FB)
2. Set 15-minute time limits (Instagram, X)
    - Add a 15-minute screen time, except have your roommate set your password
    - Everyone's tried setting their own screen time password before (news flash, it doesn't work!)
    - But imagine how your roommate might look at you if you ask them for 15 more minutes on Reels...

## YouTube 
1. Disable watch history on YouTube
    - This single change has probably saved me the most time in my life
    - When you disable your watch history, you disable the recommended videos on the home page
    - When there are no recommended videos, you have to search for what you want to watch
    - You will find that without being presented options to mindlessly click on, your YouTube experience becomes much more intentional and limited 
    - This is what my YouTube homepage looks like: 
    ![YouTube Homepage](/assets/images/yt_homepage.png)
2. Disable recommended side panel of videos on YouTube
    - Adding the following rule to AdGuard allows you to disable the side panel of recommended videos on YouTube
    ![YouTube AdGuard](/assets/images/yt_adguard.png)

## Work 
1. Block email, chat, and calendar
    - My biggest distractions at work are actually email, chat, and calendar
    - When I'm trying to focus, I disable them with this [Chrome extension](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/blocksite-block-websites/eiimnmioipafcokbfikbljfdeojpcgbh?pli=1)
    ![Block Site](/assets/images/block_site.png)
2. Lock phone away
    - I use this [phone lockbox](https://www.amazon.com/Mindsight-Personal-Distractions-Willpower-Wellness/dp/B0F56RP9BK/) from Amazon 
    - Throw your phone in, set a two-hour timer, and put the lockbox somewhere far away from your desk
    - Out of sight, out of mind
